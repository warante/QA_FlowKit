# RF-020 usability charter

## User profile

First-time leisure traveler, mobile-first, no prior experience with the booking product.

## Task

Open the booking summary after purchase and confirm itinerary, passenger name and fare total without assistance.

## Success criterion

At least 80% of participants complete the task within 5 minutes without asking for help.

## Method

Moderated remote session (60 minutes), think-aloud encouraged, facilitator notes deviations.

## Evidence

Session recordings, facilitator notes and a short findings summary linked from the traceability matrix.
